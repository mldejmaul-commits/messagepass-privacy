Privacy Policy for MessagePass

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

MessagePass respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information MessagePass collects, how it is used, how it is protected, and how you can request deletion of your account and associated data.

1. App Information

App Name: MessagePass
Developer: Md Ejmaul Hoque
Privacy Contact: mldejmaul@gmail.com

MessagePass is a messaging application designed to allow users to communicate while providing first-message screening, Silent Inbox controls, messaging limits, and user reporting features.

2. Information We Collect

When you use MessagePass, we may collect:

Account Information

- Name or display name
- Email address
- Google account user ID
- Profile photo URL, if provided through Google Sign-In

Messaging Information

- Messages that you send and receive
- Message timestamps
- Message editing information
- Screening and Silent Inbox states related to conversations

Safety and Account Information

- User reports submitted through the app
- Information necessary to maintain account security and messaging restrictions

MessagePass does not intentionally collect precise device location, contacts, microphone recordings, or camera recordings for its messaging functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

- Create and maintain your MessagePass account
- Authenticate you through Google Sign-In
- Deliver and synchronize messages
- Provide first-message screening
- Provide the Silent Inbox
- Enforce messaging limits and anti-spam protections
- Allow permitted message editing
- Process user reports
- Maintain application security
- Provide account deletion functionality

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Message Privacy

Messages are stored so they can be delivered and synchronized between intended participants.

MessagePass uses access controls designed to prevent unauthorized users from reading or modifying another user's messages.

Users should avoid sending passwords, financial information, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary messages.

5. Firebase and Google Services

MessagePass uses Google Firebase services for authentication and application data storage.

Google Sign-In is used to authenticate users. Relevant account information may be stored in Firebase/Firestore to operate the MessagePass account.

Data transmitted between the application and supported backend services is protected using secure network transport such as HTTPS/TLS.

6. Notifications

MessagePass does not use Android notification permission or system-level notification bypass mechanisms for its first-message screening system.

First-message screening and Silent Inbox interactions are handled inside the application.

7. Payments

The application may provide a 24-hour messaging pass.

During development and testing, MessagePass may use a simulated payment flow.

If a production payment provider or Google Play Billing is used, payment processing will be handled according to the applicable payment provider's policies. MessagePass does not intentionally store users' full payment card or UPI credentials.

8. Data Sharing

MessagePass does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the application, including Google/Firebase services used for authentication, database storage, and related infrastructure.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information, including authentication controls, Firestore security rules, access restrictions, secure data transmission, and server-side validation.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

Account and message information is retained while necessary to provide MessagePass services.

When an account deletion request is completed, associated account information and user data under MessagePass's control will be deleted, except where limited retention is required for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, legal, or regulatory purposes.

11. Account Deletion

Users can delete their account from inside the MessagePass application through:

Settings → Delete Account

Users who cannot access the application may request account deletion by contacting:

mldejmaul@gmail.com

When an account deletion request is completed, associated account information and application data will be deleted according to our deletion procedures.

12. User Reports

Users may report inappropriate, abusive, or spam-related activity through the application's reporting functionality.

Reports are used for safety, abuse prevention, and maintaining the integrity of the service.

13. Children's Privacy

MessagePass is intended for users aged 18 and above and is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly target children with the service.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, services, or legal requirements change.

The updated version will be made available through the MessagePass privacy policy page.
